Hawaii

Closer, warmer, and still not cheap

8 nights from Salt Lake City. Easier than Switzerland. The decision is which island, not whether we can get there.

Why

A real trip that does not eat two travel days

  • SLC to Honolulu is about 7 hours. No passport, no jet lag like Europe
  • Delta and others run this route all the time. November is the cheap month
  • Beach, hikes, food. A different kind of reset than the Alps
  • Still $5k–$10k for two once hotels and a car show up

Which island

Pick one. Do not island-hop the first time

Oahu

Easiest and cheapest. Waikiki, North Shore, hikes, food. Best first trip. Hotels around $250–$350 a night.

Maui

The classic "I want Hawaii" island. Road to Hana, Haleakala, beaches. Hotels closer to $400–$500. The spendy one.

Big Island

Volcanoes, both coasts, more driving. Mid cost, more space.

Kauai

Green, quiet, hiking. Closer to Maui on price. Smaller and slower.

When

Skip June if we care about the flight

  • SLC–Hawaii round trip is often $450–$700 per person
  • November average is about $530. June is about $750
  • Cheapest recent SLC–Honolulu round trips have been in the high $300s
  • Whale season on Maui is winter. Shoulder spring and fall are the sweet spot

A first trip

8 nights on Oahu, one hotel

  • Waikiki or a condo with a kitchen (food is the second biggest line)
  • Car for 4–5 days, not the whole week, unless we stay outside town
  • Diamond Head or Koko Head, North Shore, a snorkel day, one nicer dinner
  • If this is the dream version, swap the same shape onto Maui and add about $2,000

How to keep it sane

Condo, poke, one paid tour

  • Stay somewhere with a kitchen. Food for two is $150–$200 a day if we eat out every meal
  • Grocery poke and food trucks are the move, not resort breakfast
  • Hawaii TAT is about 14% on top of the room rate. The listed price is not the bill
  • One island. Inter-island hops look cheap and eat a day
  • Book the flight in November or shoulder spring, not June

Open questions

Decide these before booking

  • Oahu first, or go straight to Maui
  • Hotel vs condo
  • Car the whole week or only for the North Shore / Hana days
  • Just us, or this is the trip we take other people on
