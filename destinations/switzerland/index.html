Switzerland

Alps, trains, and a bill that is actually that high

10 days from Salt Lake City. A loop through Zurich, Lucerne, Interlaken, Zermatt, and Geneva. Built so I can present it, then decide if we book it.

Why this one is first

The place I keep coming back to

  • Matterhorn and Jungfrau are the picture of "I want to go there"
  • The trains are the trip, not a chore between the trip
  • Compact. You can see a lot without renting a car
  • It is one of the most expensive countries in Europe. Better to know that going in

When

June or September if I can pick

Best

June and September. Long days, fewer crowds than July and August, wildflowers or clear views.

Avoid for price

July flights from SLC run about $1,200–$1,300. February and November are cheaper, but alpine towns go quiet.

Ski trip is a different trip

February is cheaper to fly and great for snow. This deck is the sightseeing loop, not a ski week.

Flights

No nonstop SLC–Zurich. One stop, about 13–16 hours. Typical round trip $750–$1,100 in shoulder season.

The loop

10 days, two bases, not five hotels

  • Days 1–2: Zurich, then Lucerne (Chapel Bridge, lake, Pilatus or Rigi)
  • Days 3–6: Interlaken as the hub. Jungfraujoch, Lauterbrunnen, Mürren
  • Days 7–8: Zermatt. Gornergrat and the Matterhorn
  • Days 9–10: Geneva or Lake Geneva, then fly home from GVA or back through Zurich

Unpack twice. Interlaken covers both valleys. Zermatt is a clean train move.

Getting around

The pass is the whole strategy

Swiss Travel Pass

Unlimited trains, boats, city transit, a lot of museums. An 8-day 2nd class pass is about $470 per person. Pays off if we move around most days.

Half Fare Card

CHF 150 for a month, 50% off almost everything, including the big mountain lifts. Better if we sit in Interlaken and do a few expensive peaks.

Jungfraujoch is not fully covered by the Travel Pass. Glacier Express seats need a reservation months ahead.

The number

Budget couple: about $5,000–$6,500. Luxury: $16,000 and up. Per person mid-range lands around $3,500–$5,500 including flights.

What actually costs

Food and cable cars, not the flight

  • A normal restaurant plate is $30–$50. Two dinners out add up fast
  • Coop and Migros meals are about $15 and they are fine
  • A round-trip mountain lift can be $60–$220. Jungfraujoch is the expensive one
  • Hotels in Interlaken and Zermatt run $220–$380 a night for something decent
  • Always pay in CHF at the terminal. Dynamic currency conversion is a tax

How to keep it sane

Still Switzerland, less waste

  • Two bases, not five hotel check-ins
  • Half Fare Card if we are peak-hunting from Interlaken. Travel Pass if we hop cities
  • Picnic lunches. One sit-down dinner
  • Shoulder season from SLC (not July)
  • Book Glacier Express only if we want the 8-hour scenic. Direct Zermatt is 2–5 hours cheaper

Open questions

Decide these before booking

  • June/September sightseeing, or a winter ski week instead
  • Fly home from Geneva or back through Zurich
  • Travel Pass vs Half Fare Card once the daily plan is locked
  • Just us, or a longer trip that adds France or Italy
